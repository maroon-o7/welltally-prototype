Resting energy estimate

An estimate of energy used at rest—not a daily calorie target.

One stone equals 14 pounds.
— Estimated kcal used at rest per day. This is not an intake recommendation. Your age is outside the equation’s original study range, so this estimate may have greater uncertainty. These inputs produce an implausible estimate. Check the entered units and values.

For adults age 18 and older. Calculated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, originally developed using adults ages 19–78. Predictive equations can differ meaningfully from measured resting energy and may not represent every body or circumstance.
